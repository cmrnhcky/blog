<span class="lede">There is a shelf</span> in every Florida liquor store that runs from about eighteen dollars to about twenty-five, and almost nobody stops at it. Below it are the plastic handles. Above it is the wax seal and the story on the back label. The middle shelf has neither, which is exactly why it is the only part of the store where the price still means something.
