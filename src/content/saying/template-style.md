<span class="lede">Ninety dollars</span> is not a lot of money for a boot and it is a great deal of money for a bad one. ▶ Open on the object and the price in the first sentence. Not on a scene, not on a memory, not on the state of menswear. The thing, and what it costs.

▶ Now the argument. Two or three paragraphs, and each one should survive a reader who owns the competing product. No adjective does any work here that a fact could do instead.

▶ The second paragraph is where an Endorsement usually dies. Resist summarising the first. Go somewhere: how it ages, what breaks, what it replaced, what you stopped buying because of it.
