     LISTS — the shape

     This is the form to reach for when you have twenty minutes, not
     two hours. It is also the one that goes wrong most often, in
     exactly one way: explaining too much.

     THE RULES
     1. It is RANKED, not collected. "Seven good beers" is a shrug.
        "Seven beers, ranked" is a position. Number them.
     2. Two or three sentences per entry. Maximum. The confidence is
        the content — a ranked list that justifies itself at length
        is a list that doesn't believe its own order.
     3. No preamble. Do not spend a paragraph explaining why you made
        a list. Start at 1, or at the bottom if you're counting up.
     4. Number one should be slightly surprising. If the obvious
        winner wins, nobody needed the list.
     5. Odd numbers read better than even. 5, 7, 9.

     LENGTH: whatever seven short entries come to. 300-450 words.
